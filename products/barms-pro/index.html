01 Orders

Every sale recorded.

Orders for tables, the counter or open tabs, paid by cash, MoMo or card, with a printed or SMS receipt.

  • Open a tab and settle once at the end
  • Discounts follow the Owner’s rules
  • Wrong sale? A void request with a reason

02 Stock

Every bottle accounted for.

Each sale reduces stock, recipes deduct their ingredients, and crates convert into single bottles.

  • Low-stock alerts before a busy night
  • Weekly counts show each difference
  • Supplier purchases: paid and owed

03 Kitchen

The kitchen is connected.

Food orders appear on a live kitchen screen as they are taken, in order of arrival.

  • New, preparing, ready
  • Recipes deduct ingredients automatically
  • The kitchen screen needs internet

04 Offline

No internet. Still selling.

Approved devices keep taking orders and payments, then upload automatically, oldest first, with no duplicates.

  • One main device sells offline, so sellers never clash
  • Offline orders are numbered OFF-0001, OFF-0002…
  • Up to 14 days without reaching IMS

Try it · a simulation of the behaviour described

Online. Orders are saved to the server as they happen.

    05 Control

    Mistakes leave a trail.

    Each person has their own login and role. Voids and refunds need a reason and an approval, and key actions are recorded.

    • Owner, Manager, Bartender and Kitchen roles
    • Permissions the Owner switches on for managers
    • The server checks every permission on every action
    1. Wrong saleBartender sends a void request with a reason
    2. Red badgeOwner sees the pending request
    3. DecisionApprove or reject
    4. Audit logRequest and decision recorded

    06 Devices

    Install from the browser.

    No app store. Open the link on any phone, tablet or computer, install it, and sign in.

    • IMS approves each device before it reaches your data
    • A lost phone can be blocked
    • Updates install themselves
