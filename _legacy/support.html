FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from our customers.

What kind of training is included when I buy a system?
+
We provide full system usage training when we install your system. Our technician will walk your staff through every feature — POS, inventory, reports, and user management — until everyone is comfortable and confident. We do not provide general business training or consulting, only system usage training.
What do I do if my system stops working?
+
Contact us immediately via WhatsApp (+250 780 000 000) or phone. Our team will troubleshoot remotely first, and if necessary, schedule an on-site visit within 24–48 hours. For critical issues affecting your business operations, we prioritize same-day response.
Can I get support if I add new staff members who need training?
+
Yes. If you hire new staff who need to learn the system, contact us and we'll schedule a refresher training session. Basic remote guidance is available free of charge. On-site refresher sessions may incur a small service fee depending on your plan.
How often is the system updated?
+
We release system updates regularly — typically once every 1–2 months. Updates include bug fixes, security patches, and new features. All updates are applied automatically with no disruption to your business operations, and they are always free.
Is my business data safe if my computer crashes?
+
Yes. Your data is automatically backed up every day to a secure server. If your computer or hardware fails, we can restore your complete business data with minimal downtime. This backup is included in all subscription plans.
Do you provide support on weekends?
+
WhatsApp support is available 7 days a week for urgent issues. Phone support is available Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. For emergencies outside these hours, send us a WhatsApp message and we'll respond as quickly as possible.
Can I get support in Kinyarwanda or French?
+
Yes. Our support team communicates in Kinyarwanda, French, and English. You can reach us in whichever language is most comfortable for you and your staff.
