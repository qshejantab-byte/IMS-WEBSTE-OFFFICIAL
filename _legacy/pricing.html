Pricing FAQ

Common Questions About Pricing

What is included in the installation fee?
+
The 50,000 RWF installation fee covers the complete setup of your system — hardware configuration, software installation, database setup, product/menu loading, and full staff training. It is paid only once when we first install your system.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
+
Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade your subscription plan at any time. Simply contact our team and we'll adjust your plan to the next tier. The price difference will be prorated for the remaining days of your current billing month.
How do I pay my monthly subscription?
+
You can pay via MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Bank Transfer, or Cash. Our team will send you a payment reminder before each month's billing date.
Are there any other hidden fees?
+
No. The installation fee (50,000 RWF, one-time) and the monthly subscription are all you pay. There are no setup charges, no update fees, and no hidden costs. Everything is transparent from day one.
What if I want to cancel?
+
You can cancel your subscription at any time by contacting us. We don't charge cancellation fees. Your system will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
