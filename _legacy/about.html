Who We Are

Rwanda's Leading Business Management Software Company

IMS Group is a Kigali-based technology company dedicated to building smart, affordable business management systems for small and medium enterprises across Rwanda and Africa.

We specialize in Point-of-Sale (POS), inventory management, and operations software — tailored to the realities of Rwandan businesses: local payment methods, local language support, and local customer service.

Our systems are trusted by bars, restaurants, retail shops, pharmacies, and more across Rwanda.

Purpose & Direction

Our Mission & Vision

🎯
Our Mission

Digitizing Rwanda's Business Ecosystem

To empower businesses with simple, powerful, and affordable management systems — so every shop, bar, or restaurant in Rwanda can operate with the efficiency of a Fortune 500 company.

We believe technology should not be a luxury reserved for big corporations. With IMS Group, even a small retail shop in Kigali can have the same management capabilities as any global enterprise.

🌍
Our Vision

A Fully Digital Business Africa

To become the leading provider of smart business management systems across Africa — starting from Rwanda and scaling across the continent.

We envision a future where every business in Africa operates with digital efficiency, data-driven decision making, and world-class management practices enabled by accessible, affordable technology.

More Than Just Software

System Development
