Trust

No badges we have not earned.

  • 01

    No invented proof

    No certifications, audits, uptime guarantees or customer claims are made here. We publish them when they exist and can be verified.

  • 02

    Customer stories

    Published only with the customer’s agreement and verified facts. None yet. The best proof of BarMS Pro is seeing it run.

  • 03

    Personal logins

    BarMS Pro gives everyone their own login, with lockout after five wrong passwords and sign-out after 10 idle minutes.
